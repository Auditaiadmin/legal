1. About AuditAI

1.1 What is AuditAI and how does it work?

AuditAI is software with integrated generative artificial intelligence, designed exclusively for ISO ecosystems. It acts as an Intelligent Assistant that evaluates ISO documentation using the criteria of an expert auditor, helping organizations review documentary evidence, identify deviations, and obtain a structured diagnosis of how well their Management System meets the applicable requirements.

Its operation is based on the organization uploading documentation. From there, AuditAI analyzes those documents, classifies them in relation to the selected standard or standards, and generates an assessment with findings, observations, opportunities for improvement, and strengths. In this way, the organization can obtain a much clearer, faster, and more structured view of the state of its documentary system.

AuditAI is not intended as a simple document reader, but as a specialized tool that transforms documentation into useful information for decision-making, action prioritization, and audit preparation.

1.2 What problem does AuditAI solve?

AuditAI solves one of the most common challenges in ISO system management: the manual review of documentation. In many organizations, this process is time-consuming, requires expert resources, and can become inconsistent when several people are involved or when the documentary volume is high.

The tool makes it possible to reduce that manual effort, detect deviations before an audit, and review documentation using a more consistent methodology. This helps reduce rework, prioritize actions, and improve the overall readiness of the system.

1.3 Does AuditAI replace an auditor or a Management System manager?

No. AuditAI does not replace either the auditor or the Management System manager. The tool is designed to act as expert support, accelerating and structuring the documentary review, but the final interpretation and decision-making still depend on human judgment.

In other words, AuditAI helps people work better and faster, but it does not eliminate the need for professional supervision and validation.

1.4 Does AuditAI replace a certification audit?

No. AuditAI does not replace an audit carried out by a Certification Body, nor is that its purpose. Its value lies in helping the organization arrive better prepared for that moment, with a prior review that is deeper, more organized, and more agile.

It can be used to detect possible deviations before the formal audit, but the certification audit will still be conducted by the relevant body.

1.5 What makes AuditAI different from a general-purpose AI?

AuditAI is not a general-purpose artificial intelligence tool adapted to the ISO environment. It is a solution developed from the ground up for a specific purpose: to assess Management System evidence using the same criteria applied by an expert auditor from a certification body.

These are the most relevant differences compared with a general-purpose AI:

Audit logic specifically built for ISO management systems, not generic. AuditAI applies the same analysis logic used by certification body auditors when reviewing documentary evidence. This is not a superficial adaptation: the tool was developed and validated by senior auditors with real experience in certification bodies, resulting in assessment criteria aligned with what is actually required in an audit.

Results aligned with the expectations of a certification body. The findings, classifications, and corrective action plans generated by AuditAI are structured according to the standard used by certification bodies. The organization does not just obtain a diagnosis: it obtains a result in the format and with the rigor that any external auditor will recognize.

Greater documentary accuracy thanks to intelligent classification. AuditAI incorporates a document classification system that makes it possible to work with a larger volume of documentation while maintaining analytical consistency. This reduces the risk of hallucinations and improves assessment accuracy, something that is not guaranteed in general-purpose tools when working with extensive or complex documentation.

Multiple layers of control to minimize errors. AuditAI’s architecture includes specific controls and safeguards designed to reduce incorrect responses or responses not grounded in real evidence. The goal is for every finding to have documentary support, not just to sound plausible.

Your documentation remains yours. Unlike many general-purpose AI tools, documents uploaded to AuditAI are not used to train models and are not shared with third parties. They are handled confidentially, stored in secure environments, and accessible only to users authorized by your own organization.

When a new standard is incorporated into the system, its availability will be communicated through the appropriate channels.

AuditAI is prepared to adapt to future revisions of standards. The goal is for organizations to continue working in an environment aligned with the evolution of standards, while respecting the applicable transition periods and the corresponding regulatory framework.

This helps reduce the operational impact of regulatory changes and facilitates a more orderly update of the system.

Each update will be communicated to current users and through AuditAI’s official communication channels.

3. How AuditAI improves ISO audits

3.1 How does AuditAI improve ISO audits?

AuditAI improves the audit process because it automates a large part of the prior documentary review. This reduces the time needed to analyze evidence, helps detect deviations in advance, and brings a more consistent structure to the diagnosis.

As a result, the organization can spend less time manually reviewing large volumes of documentation and more time understanding, correcting, and improving what truly matters.

3.2 What types of findings can AuditAI detect?

AuditAI can identify different types of findings, including:

  • Major Nonconformities
  • Minor Nonconformities
  • Observations
  • Opportunities for improvement
  • Strengths

The organization only needs to upload the documentation, define the applicable ISO standard or standards, and establish the scope it wants to assess. From there, AuditAI generates a diagnosis with clear, actionable findings that helps understand the status of the Management System and prioritize the next steps.

AuditAI not only identifies the problem: it also provides guidance on how to act and why. For each Nonconformity, it analyzes possible causes and generates a proposed resolution and corrective actions, making it easier to prepare improvement plans and make decisions.

In addition, the assessment helps structure each finding, guide its understanding, and turn the diagnosis into a practical working basis, both for correcting deviations and for reinforcing strengths and advancing continuous improvement.

3.3 Can it detect problems before an external audit?

Yes. One of AuditAI’s main benefits is precisely the early detection of possible documentary deviations. This makes it possible to act before a certification audit, reducing the risk of reaching that stage with unidentified nonconformities.

3.4 Does AuditAI assess the whole standard or only specific parts?

It can do both. The organization can use AuditAI to assess the full set of a standard or focus on specific requirements, sub-requirements, or blocks, depending on its priorities and the capacity available in the subscribed plan.

This enables both broad reviews and more focused partial analyses.

3.5 Can I review only specific documents?

Yes. It is not mandatory to upload the whole documentary system from the start. The organization can begin with key documents, higher-risk processes, or specific requirements and expand the review progressively.

In addition, depending on the use case, it is also possible to add expert notes that complement the documentation or the requirement being assessed. This is especially useful, for example, in an internal audit, when the auditor or responsible person wants to incorporate additional context, clarifications, interpretation criteria, or relevant information that is not fully reflected in the uploaded documentary evidence.

This approach makes gradual implementation easier, reduces the barrier to adopting the tool, and allows the assessment to be enriched with expert knowledge when the process requires it.

4. Accuracy, reliability, and human control

4.1 How accurate and reliable is the AI assessment?

AuditAI is designed to produce consistent and reliable assessments within its scope of use. Its approach is focused on working with ISO requirements and real documentary evidence, using a specialized analysis logic.

The goal is not to offer a generic answer, but a useful, structured, and repeatable assessment that supports the review of the Management System.

4.2 What does it mean that AuditAI prevents hallucinations?

In artificial intelligence, a hallucination is an invented, incorrect, or unsupported answer, even if it sounds convincing. AuditAI is designed to reduce that risk through controls, constraints, and an architecture aimed at working with real documentation and regulatory requirements.

This does not eliminate the value of final human validation, but it does reduce the risk of arbitrary or invented responses typical of non-specialized tools.

4.3 Can the tool make mistakes?

Like any advanced technology, the tool should be understood as support and not as a substitute for professional judgment. AuditAI is designed to deliver robust and useful results, but the organization still remains responsible for reviewing and validating the final outcome when necessary.

4.4 Can I review, refine, or correct the results?

Yes. AuditAI allows findings to be reviewed, complemented, annotated, requirements to be reassessed, and human control to be maintained throughout the process. This makes the tool a flexible aid rather than a closed box.

4.5 Is it advisable to keep a final human review?

Yes. Best practice is usually to combine AuditAI’s speed and consistency with the final review of a responsible person. This approach allows organizations to take full advantage of automation without losing control or technical judgment.

5. Security, privacy, and compliance

5.1 Is my data secure?

Yes. AuditAI applies technical and organizational measures aimed at protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and security of the information processed on the platform. Documentation is stored in secure environments and access is restricted to authorized users.

In addition, platform data is stored in AWS, which helps keep information in a recognized cloud infrastructure aligned with the needs of clients operating in the European environment.

5.2 Where is my organization’s data stored?

The data is stored in AWS. This allows the information to be hosted in cloud infrastructure located within European territory, which is especially relevant for organizations seeking to work with providers and environments aligned with the European regulatory framework.

5.3 Does AuditAI comply with GDPR?

Yes. AuditAI is designed to operate in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), applying measures aimed at protecting personal and business data.

This implies that information must be processed under principles such as confidentiality, purpose limitation, minimization, and control by the user organization, in accordance with the applicable regulations.

5.4 How is AuditAI aligned with the EU AI Act?

AuditAI is designed with a responsible, controlled, and professionally oriented use approach, aligned with the principles of the European regulatory framework on artificial intelligence.

In particular, AuditAI is oriented toward the use of AI applied to documentation, structured review, human oversight, and a defined business context, avoiding open-ended approaches or uncontrolled uses. In addition, its design seeks to reinforce relevant elements within the context of the EU AI Act, such as traceability of use, human oversight, reduction of unsupported responses, and the use of information linked to real evidence. The EU AI Regulation is already being gradually rolled out, with obligations that began to apply in 2025 and broader application expected in August 2026.

5.5 Are uploaded documents used to train the AI?

No. Documents uploaded by the organization are not used to train AI models, nor are they shared with third parties for that or any other purpose.

The information is used exclusively to provide the service requested by the organization within the platform, that is, to analyze documentation, assess it against ISO requirements, and generate the corresponding diagnoses.

5.6 What exactly are the documents I upload to AuditAI used for?

The documents are used exclusively to perform the functions of the subscribed service, such as document classification, assessment against the selected standard’s requirements, and the generation of diagnoses, findings, and associated guidance.

This means that the documentation is used to provide the service to the organization and not for purposes unrelated to the use of the platform.

5.7 Who can see the uploaded documentation?

Only users authorized by the organization itself, according to the permissions and access settings configured within the platform.

In this way, the organization maintains control over who can access the information and what level of visibility each user has within the environment.

5.8 Can the AuditAI team access my documentation?

Access to documentation must be restricted and controlled. In general terms, the information is intended for use by the organization itself through its authorized users.

If, in an exceptional case, technical access were required for support, maintenance, or incident resolution reasons, such access would have to take place under internal controls, for a limited purpose, and in accordance with the service conditions and applicable regulations.

5.9 How are accesses controlled within the platform?

AuditAI restricts access to information to authorized users. Access and permission management allows the visibility of documentation and the working environment to be limited according to each user’s role or level of involvement, when this is defined in the service configuration.

5.10 Is data shared with third parties?

The organization’s documents are not shared with third parties for model training or for purposes unrelated to the subscribed service.

However, as with most SaaS platforms, technological providers necessary for providing the service may be involved, for example cloud infrastructure providers. In this case, storage in AWS in Europe forms part of the service architecture.

5.11 Does AuditAI work with external providers or subprocessors?

Yes, to the extent that every SaaS platform needs to rely on certain technology providers to deliver the service, such as cloud infrastructure providers.

In the case of AuditAI, data is stored in AWS in Europe. The participation of this type of provider should be understood within the technical framework necessary to operate the platform and under the corresponding contractual and data protection conditions.

Yes. The organization may request the deletion of its data in accordance with the service conditions and applicable regulations.

5.16 What approach does AuditAI follow regarding the responsible use of artificial intelligence?

AuditAI is designed for professional and bounded use of AI, applied to the assessment of ISO documentation and not to open interactions without context.

Its approach prioritizes work on real documentation, human oversight, traceability of analysis, and the reduction of unsupported responses. This approach fits with the direction of the European AI framework, whose progressive rollout aims to strengthen transparency, governance, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence systems in the EU.

6. AuditAI use cases

6.1 In which use cases does AuditAI provide the most value?

AuditAI can add value in a wide variety of situations. Some of the most common use cases are the following:

Use case: faster and more structured internal audits
AuditAI helps speed up the preparation of internal audits, especially when the team has little time or when there are many documents to review. The platform helps structure the analysis and focus attention on possible deviations.

Use case: preparation for a certification audit
When an organization is approaching an external audit, AuditAI helps review documentation beforehand, detect possible weaknesses, and organize the response better before the certification body intervenes.

This reduces the risk of arriving with incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficiently reviewed evidence.

Use case: organizations with multiple sites
In multisite structures, documentary review can be especially complex. AuditAI helps centralize the analysis, better coordinate teams, and review documentation from different locations using a more consistent methodology.

Use case: integrated systems
When an organization works with several standards, the review is usually more demanding. AuditAI adds value by helping manage that complexity and review documentation with more coherent logic across requirements.

Use case: teams with several users involved
When several people participate in preparing the system, uploading documentation, or reviewing findings, AuditAI facilitates collaboration on a single centralized platform.

Use case: periodic system review throughout the year
AuditAI is not only useful before an audit. It can also be used as a continuous review tool to keep the system updated, review documentary changes, and detect deviations earlier throughout the year.

Use case: support for quality managers with limited time
In many organizations, the quality or system manager works with limited resources. AuditAI helps reduce the manual review burden and focus time on decisions, corrections, and follow-up.

Use case: support for consultants or external teams
Consultants and advisors can also find value in AuditAI as a support tool to review documentation, prepare follow-up meetings, or strengthen their clients’ readiness before audits.

8. Plans and subscriptions

8.1 How do AuditAI plans work?

AuditAI plans are designed to adapt to different levels of organizational complexity. Not all organizations have the same documentary volume, the same number of users, or the same regulatory scope, so the platform structures its offering to support different usage scenarios.

In general, plans vary according to aspects such as:

  • number of automatic assessments included
  • number of users
  • recommended scope according to organizational size and complexity
  • ability to work with one or several standards
  • level of guidance or customization

8.2 What is an automatic assessment?

An automatic assessment corresponds to the assessment of a requirement, sub-requirement, or defined set of requirements from an ISO standard. Each automatic assessment represents 1 credit unit, which is how usage is measured within the subscribed plan.

In this way, credits work as the platform’s usage unit and allow the organization to distribute its capacity flexibly, both for full reviews and for partial analyses of specific requirements, according to its needs.
